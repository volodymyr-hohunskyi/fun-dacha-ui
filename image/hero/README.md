- **banner-1.jpg** – Slide 1 product/hero image
- **banner-2.jpg** – Slide 2 product/hero image  
- **banner-3.jpg** – Slide 3 product/hero image
- **banner-4.jpg** – Slide 4 product/hero image
- **banner-5.jpg** – Slide 5 product/hero image

Recommended size: 600×500px or similar for slide images. Background: full-width (e.g. 1920×600).
